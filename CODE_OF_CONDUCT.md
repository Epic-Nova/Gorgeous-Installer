# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We welcome contributors and users regardless of background or experience. Be respectful, constructive, and considerate in discussions, reviews, and issue reports.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good intent and communicate clearly.
- Offer actionable technical feedback.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing credentials or personal information.
- Help keep the project welcoming and focused.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to [support@epicnova.net](mailto:support@epicnova.net). Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation as needed.
